# Welcome to the Tegh private beta

Thank you for testing Tegh. This beta is **invitation-only and free**, and runs for about **two weeks**.

> **Use sample data only.** Do not enter real clients, employees, bank accounts or tax numbers. Beta data may be reset at any time. Tegh is not yet for filing taxes or running payroll; keep your own records as usual.

Support: **[support email]** · Report problems with the feedback form: **[link]**

---

## 1. Create your account (5 minutes)
1. Open the email from Tegh. It contains a **10-character invitation code** (for example `AB3CD-7EFGH`).
2. Go to **[beta address]/app.html** and choose the **Create account** tab.
3. Enter **the same email address the invitation was sent to** and the code.
4. Choose your name and a strong password. Accept the beta terms and privacy notice.
5. You are signed in. A yellow line under the top bar reminds you that this is a beta with sample data.

The code works once. If it has expired or you made a typo too many times, ask us for a new invitation.

## 2. Make a sample company (5 minutes)
1. Choose **Add Company**. Use an invented name, for example "Maple Test Studio".
2. Pick the province, fiscal year end (31 December is fine) and the start of the books (1 January 2026).
3. Answer the sales-tax questions as if you were registered for GST/HST. Tegh creates starter tax codes.
4. Optionally add an address and a **Short Name** (for example "MTS"). The short name labels report lines when you view several companies at once.

## 3. Try the main workflows (about 30 minutes)
Do these in order with invented names and round amounts, so you can check the results yourself.

| # | Where | What to do | What you should see |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receivables › Create Customer | Add "Lakeview Test Dental" | the customer appears in Customers |
| 2 | Receivables › Customer Invoices | Invoice Lakeview: 2 × $250.00 consulting with HST, **Save & Issue** | total $565.00 in Ontario; the invoice PDF opens |
| 3 | Receivables › Customer Payments | Record $565.00 received against that invoice | the invoice shows Paid |
| 4 | Payables › Create Vendor, then Vendor Invoices | A supplier invoice of $113.00 including HST for office supplies | it appears in the Vendor Invoice Register |
| 5 | Banking › Upload Statement | Upload `sample-statement.csv` from this kit (three lines) | a preview with 3 rows; **Continue with import** |
| 6 | Banking › Match And Post Transactions | Match or post each bank line | every line is matched or posted once |
| 7 | Upload the same statement again | | every row is marked **Duplicate** and the import is disabled |
| 8 | Reports › Profit and Loss, Trial Balance | Look at this year | the Trial Balance difference is $0.00; income and expenses match what you entered |
| 9 | Payables › Document Intake | Upload a sample supplier invoice PDF (make one, or ask us) | Tegh reads the vendor, date, lines and total; correct anything wrong, then open it in the vendor invoice form |
| 10 | Top bar | Try the **sun / moon / screen** icons, and a phone | light, dark and system themes; the menu works on a phone |

Optional:
- Make a second sample company, then select **both** in the company selector at the top. Reports then show a **Co.** column.
- Budgets and the GL Account Report also work across both companies.
- Payroll Support: add a **fictional** employee (no SIN is asked for), run a pay calculation, and read the review step. Payroll Support covers Canada outside Quebec only. Do not use it for real pay.

## 4. When something looks wrong
Fill in the feedback form ([link]) with:
1. the screen (the title at the top of the page);
2. the steps you took;
3. what you expected;
4. what happened instead;
5. a screenshot, if you can (it must not show real data).

Urgent problems, such as being unable to sign in or seeing another company's data, go **by email to [support email] straight away**.

## 5. Good to know
- **Several companies.** Combined views are read-only. To change records, select one company.
- **Forgot your password?** Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The email link works once, and other signed-in devices are signed out.
- **Themes.** "System" follows your computer's or phone's light/dark setting.
- **Exports.** Most reports have **Actions › Export** (PDF, Excel, CSV).
- **Your data.** Read the beta privacy notice ([link]). Ask us any time to export or delete your test records.

Thank you. Your notes decide what gets fixed before Tegh opens to more people.
