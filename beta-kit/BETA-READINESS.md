## Before the first invitation (R156, still applies in R162)
1. Fill in the `operator` section of the beta host's `config.php`. Until every field is set, invitations are refused and both pages say "This notice is incomplete".
2. Open `/terms.html` and `/privacy.html` on the beta host and read them as a tester would.
3. Check that `http://<your beta address>/` redirects to `https://`.

The owner's eight review findings are answered in the QA report's R157 addendum. The R157 review's two backup items and the company-deletion report are answered in its R158 addendum.

**Upgrading from R160:** back up first and upload the whole package (no database change). **From R159:** if you already set `RewriteBase /api/` in `api/.htaccess` by hand, the packaged file is the same change. **Earlier, from R158:** back up first; confirm the database allows Tegh to add two columns to `companies` and the table `company_deletion_requests` (first use); upload the whole package; make sure `operator.support_email` in config.php is a mailbox you read, because company deletion requests are sent there.
