SR AccounTax Bank Statement Converter — v11 update (2026-10-06)
================================================================

v11 puts the statement reader of Tegh R152 into the website converter (free
and Pro). It includes everything in v10 (see INSTALL-v10.txt).

WHAT'S NEW
1. More statement layouts read by column, in English and French:
   - RBC-, BMO-, Scotia-, Desjardins-, National Bank- and Tangerine-style
     bank accounts;
   - Amex-style and French Visa card statements;
   - column headings printed over two lines ("Cheques &" / "Debits");
   - French amounts ("1 234,56"), French month names ("02 janv.");
   - numeric dates (01/02): the day/month order is taken from the statement
     period. When both orders fit, the row dates are left for you to set with
     Date format instead of guessing;
   - DR/CR columns and overdrawn (OD) balances;
   - amounts on the line under the description, and amounts split into
     pieces.

   On the 13 synthetic test statements (one per style above, plus a PDF that
   holds three statements):
     v10: 2 read exactly, 2 gave no rows, 1 was refused, 8 had wrong amounts
     v11: all 13 read exactly (every row, the net, opening and closing
          balances), with the statement check passing.
   The owner's three real statements (CIBC chequing, TD Visa, TD chequing)
   give the same rows and checks as v10: 135, 21 and 254 rows, all
   balanced. The earlier test statements (bank, card, page totals, missing
   line, ISO dates, no headings, scanned) read the same as v10.

2. A PDF that holds several statements (more months, or more accounts) is no
   longer refused with "This PDF contains more than one account". Each
   statement is read and checked on its own and labelled
   "file.pdf (statement 2 of 3, account …4567)". Consecutive statements of
   the same account are chained (closing balance → next opening balance).

3. Fixed: the chain check skipped statements when statements of another
   account came between them by date (for example January …4567,
   January …9012, February …4567 uploaded together).

4. Converter build 11.0.0. Cache versions are bumped to 20261006-11.

INSTALL
1. Back up the converter folder.
2. Upload the files above, overwriting the old ones.
3. Open the converter in a private window, or press Ctrl+F5.
4. Check:
   - Convert a text PDF statement. The green "Statement check passed" panel
     appears above the totals.
   - Convert a PDF that holds two months of one account. Both statements are
     listed in the panel, with a line saying the closing balance carries
     forward.

LIMITS
- Statements with no column headings use the previous reader. The panel then
  says there is nothing to check against.
- The layouts are synthetic copies of common styles. Real statements from
  other banks may still need the Column layout setting or a review of rows.
- OCR uses the bundled English model.
- Review every row before importing. The converter makes no accounting
  entries.
