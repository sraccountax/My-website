SR AccounTax Bank Statement Converter — v10 update (2026-10-01)
================================================================

WHAT'S NEW
1. Column-aware statement reader (bank-statement-layout.js). It reads where
   every word sits on the page:
   - columns: withdrawals / deposits / amount / balance; transaction and
     posting dates;
   - a day's date carried down to the lines below it;
   - wrapped descriptions joined;
   - side panels ignored, and sub-totals and payment slips skipped;
   - years taken from the statement period, so a December to January card
     statement gets the right year on each line;
   - credit cards read with charges as money out and payments as money in.

   Three real Canadian business statements supplied by the owner:
     Statement        v9          v10
     CIBC chequing    21 rows     135 rows
     TD Visa          0 rows      21 rows
     TD chequing      0 rows      254 rows
   Each v10 result matches the statement's opening and closing balances to
   the cent.

2. Statement check panel (green / amber) above the totals. It checks:
   - opening balance + rows = the closing balance printed on the statement;
   - every running balance on the statement agrees. A row that differs is
     marked and appears under "Needs review";
   - per-page totals ("Debits n" / "Credits n") where the bank prints them;
   - CHAIN CHECK: consecutive statements of the same account must carry the
     closing balance forward as the next opening balance, so a missing
     statement is flagged.
   On the free plan the whole statement is checked, even though only a
   sample is shown.

3. Scanned statements (Pro OCR):
   - Fixed: OCR failed with "getOrInsertComputed is not a function" in most
     browsers. The bundled pdf.js 6.1 needs a 2026 JavaScript method; it is
     now added where missing, on the page (pdf-upsert-polyfill.js) and in the
     PDF worker (pdf-worker-entry.mjs).
   - OCR now keeps each word's position, so the column reader and the
     balance check also work on scans.
   - Pages are rendered at a higher resolution, and faint scans get a
     contrast boost.

4. New "Account type" setting: Auto detect, Bank account, or Credit card /
   line of credit.

5. Exports:
   - NEW QuickBooks Web Connect (.qbo).
   - OFX uses the credit card format for card statements, and its ledger
     balance comes from the statement.
   - Excel has a new "Statement Check" sheet.
   - JSON and the PDF report include the statement check.

6. Converter build 10.0.0. Cache versions are bumped to 20261001-10.

INSTALL
1. Back up the converter folder.
2. Upload the files above, overwriting the old ones.
3. Open the converter in a private window, or press Ctrl+F5.
4. Check:
   - Convert a text PDF statement. The green "Statement check passed" panel
     appears above the totals.
   - Pro: tick OCR and convert a scanned statement. There is no
     "getOrInsertComputed" error.
   - Pro: Export > QuickBooks Web Connect (.qbo) downloads a file.

LIMITS
- Statements with no column headings use the previous reader. The panel then
  says there is nothing to check against.
- OCR uses the bundled English model.
- Review every row before importing. The converter makes no accounting
  entries.
