Cookies & Privacy Choices

Necessary browser storage and the optional tracking state.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-21-beta.1 · Contact: enquiry@sraccountax.ca

Current technologies

TEGH uses necessary secure sessions, CSRF protection and a recipient session for Client Dashboard access. Browser local storage may keep interface preferences such as theme or selected company. These are not advertising profiles.

Optional measurement

Optional analytics and advertising are disabled. No Meta Pixel or Google Analytics was added. Optional aggregate marketing events are disabled by configuration and the public script sends none.

Choices

No decorative banner is shown because optional tracking is inactive. Clearing browser storage signs you out or resets preferences. Future optional tracking must remain off until affirmative choice, offer equal rejection and stop scripts/requests upon withdrawal.
