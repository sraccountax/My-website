Privacy Notice

Updated October 1, 2026

Information processed

Tegh processes account details, company records, accounting transactions, payroll information (excluding Social Insurance Numbers), uploaded supporting files, audit events, account-setup delivery records and support requests.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate company files, deliver password-setup emails, prepare reports, maintain audit history, create backups, provide support and protect the service.

Company access

Company owners decide who can access their company and assign each user a role. Support access is not automatic; it must be granted by a company owner, is time-limited and can be revoked.

Client viewing links

Company owners and admins can share a view-only dashboard and selected reports with a named client. Tegh uses the client’s email address to deliver the link and sign-in codes, and records sign-ins, report views and downloads in audit history. Links expire and can be replaced or turned off. Payroll, audit history and working papers cannot be shared.

Built-in intelligence and document reading

Tegh Intelligence computes insights from company books on the Tegh server without calling an external AI service. Insight dismissals are recorded in audit history. Document Intake extracts candidate fields locally in the browser; saving or linking a source document uses the company’s document workflow. Review extracted fields before creating records.

Service records

Security, audit, email-delivery and error records may be retained to protect accounts, preserve accounting integrity, diagnose failures and meet applicable obligations. Passwords are stored using one-way password hashing.

Retention

Company data remains available while the company file and account are active, subject to authorised deletion and applicable retention requirements. Signed backups downloaded by users are controlled by the person who stores them.

Your choices

Use a unique password, review user roles and pending account setups, revoke access that is no longer needed, maintain current backups and avoid including unnecessary personal information in support requests.

Website Conversion Measurement

Tegh records aggregate counts for a small set of website actions such as beta sign-up starts, demo-request progress, pricing views and migration-page views. This launch measurement does not store names, email addresses, user IDs, raw IP addresses, cookies or accounting data in the event counter.

Security

Bot and abuse protection

Tegh may use Cloudflare Turnstile on account-registration and suspicious sign-in attempts to distinguish legitimate users from automated abuse. The CAPTCHA secret stays on the Tegh server; only the public site key and challenge token are used in the browser.
