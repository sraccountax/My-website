Privacy Policy

How TEGH handles personal information for the service, beta operation and customer-instructed accounting work.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-21-beta.1 · Contact: enquiry@sraccountax.ca

1. Responsibility

TEGH is operated under SR Accountax, 41 Dufay Rd, Brampton, Ontario L7A 4A2, Canada. Privacy Officer: Ravinder Singh, Owner. Email: enquiry@sraccountax.ca.

SR Accountax determines how account, security, support and administration information is used. For accounting, payroll, employee, customer, supplier, message and uploaded-document content, the customer determines the business purpose and instructs TEGH to process it.

2. Information and sources

Information comes from users, authorized customer users, service operation and security events. It can include names/contact details; credentials and MFA status; company settings; accounting records; customer/supplier data; invoices and bills; bank-statement data; payroll and employee records; documents/messages/requests/acknowledgements; support/privacy requests; session and IP-derived security data; audit events; and agreement records.

Current payroll rejects new SIN entry and clears identified legacy SIN fields. Do not put government identifiers, passwords or unnecessary sensitive data in free text, ordinary email or public forms.

3. Purposes

Purposes are to provide and secure accounts; keep companies separate; operate accounting, payroll, document and Client Dashboard features; send requested transactional messages; preserve audit/consent evidence; respond to support and rights requests; detect incidents/abuse; create customer-directed exports/backups; and meet legal duties. Optional marketing permission is separate. Optional analytics and advertising are disabled.

4. Consent and choices

Terms and this policy are available before registration; accepted versions and time are recorded. Required processing is limited to operating and protecting the service. Future marketing permission must be optional, unselected by default and withdrawable. Withdrawal from processing required for a feature may require disabling it or closing the account.

5. Providers and international processing

Production hosting is intended on IONOS infrastructure in the United States. Data may be processed there and subject to lawful United States access. Owner-authorized TEGH administration is limited to Ravinder Singh, but this does not exclude infrastructure operations, automated systems, contracted providers or compelled access.

Email and backup providers must be confirmed before use. Optional Cloudflare Turnstile and external OpenAI processing are disabled unless separately configured/reviewed. Current document extraction runs in the browser and raw OCR text is not intentionally stored. External AI must not be enabled until its provider, location, purpose, retention and model-training terms are disclosed and approved. See Subprocessors.

6. Safeguards

Application controls include password hashing, MFA-capable authentication, session controls, company/role authorization, recipient verification, private file handling, upload allowlists/limits, audit events, period locks and balanced posting. These are design and local-evidence statements, not certification. Host TLS, database privileges, backups and restoration require deployment verification.

7. Retention, export and closure

Customers can request export and closure. Proposed, pending approval: disable access on closure; a 30-day export window; primary deletion within a further 30 days unless duties, disputes, security needs or legal holds require retention; routine backup expiry within 35 days. Customer accounting/payroll retention depends on customer legal duties. Audit, agreement, privacy-request and incident records may remain longer. No new irreversible deletion job is enabled.

8. Rights and complaints

Email the Privacy Officer for access, correction, optional-consent withdrawal, closure questions or complaints. Provide only enough information to locate the account. We may verify control of the registered email/account and avoid identity documents unless necessary. Requests are logged with a target date. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or applicable provincial regulator.

9. Incidents and changes

We assess incidents and provide legally required notices. Canadian breach duties are risk-based; this policy does not promise a universal 72-hour deadline. Material policy changes are dated/versioned with notice and renewed choice where required. Paid plans receive at least 30 days’ notice and require express choice.
