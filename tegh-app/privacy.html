Privacy Notice

Updated October 8, 2026

Who is responsible

This Tegh service is operated by , , which is responsible for the personal information described here. Privacy questions and requests: .

Your own information as a user or beta tester

Your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), sign-in sessions, the terms and privacy version you accepted and the actions you take are real personal information, even during a beta that uses sample accounting data. Sign-in and abuse protection keep hashed (not readable) network and browser identifiers; failed sign-in records are deleted after 2 days and password-reset requests after 2 days.

Information processed

Tegh processes account details, company records, accounting transactions, payroll information (excluding Social Insurance Numbers), uploaded supporting files, audit events, account-setup delivery records and support requests.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate company files, deliver password-setup emails, prepare reports, maintain audit history, create backups, provide support and protect the service.

Company access

Company owners decide who can access their company and assign each user a role. Support access is not automatic; it must be granted by a company owner, is time-limited and can be revoked.

Client viewing links

Company owners and admins can share a view-only dashboard and selected reports with a named client. Tegh uses the client’s email address to deliver the link and sign-in codes, and records sign-ins, report views and downloads in audit history. Links expire and can be replaced or turned off. Payroll, audit history and working papers cannot be shared.

Built-in intelligence and document reading

Tegh Intelligence computes insights from company books on the Tegh server without calling an external AI service. Insight dismissals are recorded in audit history. Document Intake extracts candidate fields locally in the browser; saving or linking a source document uses the company’s document workflow. Review extracted fields before creating records.

Service records

Security, audit, email-delivery and error records may be retained to protect accounts, preserve accounting integrity, diagnose failures and meet applicable obligations. Passwords are stored using one-way password hashing.

Where information is stored and who processes it

Tegh runs on servers provided by , with data stored in . Daily backups are kept by the operator, with a copy at . Emails (invitations, password resets, client sign-in codes) are delivered through , which receives the recipient address and message. When bot protection is enabled, Cloudflare processes the browser check described below. No other service receives your information: Tegh uses no third-party analytics, advertising or session-recording tools, and no external AI service.

Retention

Company data remains available while the company file and account are active, subject to authorised deletion and applicable retention requirements. During the private beta, beta records may be reset or deleted, including at the end of the beta. Operator backups are kept for days, so deleted information can remain in a backup until that backup expires. Signed backups downloaded by users are controlled by the person who stores them.

Access, correction and deletion

You can correct your name and change your password in Tegh. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account, email from the address on your account. The operator confirms the request comes from you and replies within days. Deleting an account removes your name, email address, password, sign-in sessions and company access, and replaces your email address in invitation, email-delivery and service records. The tamper-evident audit history of a company you worked in keeps the email address you used there for as long as that company exists, because each entry is protected by a hash; deleting the company removes it. Tegh keeps the anonymised login and a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the deleted email address, not the address itself. If the same address is invited back, the operator can restore that earlier login instead of creating a new one; Tegh then emails you that the login was restored, with a link to choose a new password. The company access it had is not restored. Company owners can export a company (signed backup, PDF, Excel and CSV) before deletion.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects personal information, the operator will investigate, contain it, and notify affected people and the appropriate authorities where the law requires.

Your choices

Use a unique password, review user roles and pending account setups, revoke access that is no longer needed, maintain current backups and avoid including unnecessary personal information in support requests.

Website Conversion Measurement

The public website counts a fixed list of nine actions per day (for example beta sign-up starts, demo-request steps, pricing views and migration-page views). Tegh does this itself, on its own server: only a daily total for each action is kept, with no names, email addresses, user IDs, IP addresses, cookies or accounting data, and nothing is sent to an analytics company.

Security

Bot and abuse protection

Tegh may use Cloudflare Turnstile on account-registration and suspicious sign-in attempts to distinguish legitimate users from automated abuse. The CAPTCHA secret stays on the Tegh server; only the public site key and challenge token are used in the browser.
