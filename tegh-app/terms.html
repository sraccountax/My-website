Beta Terms of Use

Terms for free beta access. These drafts do not activate a paid plan.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-21-beta.1 · Contact: enquiry@sraccountax.ca

1. Operator and agreement

These terms are supplied for TEGH by SR Accountax, 41 Dufay Rd, Brampton, Ontario L7A 4A2, Canada. Privacy Officer: Ravinder Singh, Owner. Email: enquiry@sraccountax.ca.

Registration records the version accepted. The owner must confirm before approval whether SR Accountax is the full legal contracting person or Ravinder Singh’s trade name.

2. Beta and pricing

TEGH is currently in beta and free for users. No beta end date is promised. At least 30 days’ notice will precede paid plans. You will not be charged or converted unless you expressly choose a paid subscription.

3. Responsibilities

Protect credentials/MFA, limit roles, remove former users, report compromise and have authority for data you provide. Configure TEGH consistently with your recordkeeping, employment, tax and privacy duties.

4. Scope and review

Worldwide access does not provide worldwide legal, tax, payroll, filing or remittance support. Canadian accounting/payroll are the current focus; Quebec payroll is unsupported. Direct bank connections are coming soon. Independently review material outputs, filings, deductions and payments.

5. Client Dashboard

Administrators choose recipients, customer invoice scope and capabilities. Acknowledgement records only the displayed response and never posts entries, authorizes payments or grants additional authority.

6. Acceptable use

Do not bypass access controls, probe other accounts, upload malware, violate law, or place unnecessary identifiers/credentials in text or uploads. Security testing requires prior written authorization.

7. Availability and exit

The beta has no uptime or support SLA. Keep source records/exports. We may suspend access for abuse, risk, legal duties or beta operation and provide reasonable notice when circumstances permit.

8. Data and intellectual property

You retain rights in customer data and grant only rights needed to host/process/back up/transmit it for the requested service. TEGH software and branding remain with their owners.

9. Legal review items

The beta is provided “as available” to the extent allowed by law, without certification, filing acceptance or error-free results. Final warranties, liability, indemnity, governing law and dispute language require Canadian legal review.

10. Changes and contact

Email enquiry@sraccountax.ca. Changes are versioned and notified as appropriate; material new uses and paid service require the choices above.
