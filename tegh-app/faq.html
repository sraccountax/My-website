Frequently asked questions

What does beta cost?

TEGH is currently in beta and free for users. At least 30 days’ notice will be provided before paid plans. You must expressly choose a paid subscription; beta use will not automatically become a charge.

Does TEGH connect directly to banks?

Not yet. Direct bank connections are coming soon. The current workflow imports CSV, XLSX and supported PDF statements for review, matching and reconciliation.

What can clients do?

An authorized administrator can give a recipient-verified client scoped access to invoices, documents, requests, acknowledgements, messages and selected reports. An acknowledgement cannot post an entry or authorize a payment.

Can I access TEGH outside Canada?

The service is intended to be accessible worldwide. That does not mean local tax, payroll, filing, remittance or legal rules are supported everywhere.

Where is data intended to be hosted?

The owner selected IONOS hosting in the United States. Information may be processed under United States law and accessible to infrastructure providers or authorities where legally required.

Does TEGH use advertising or analytics?

Optional analytics and advertising are disabled in this release. Necessary session/security technologies and local interface preferences remain.

How do I get help?

Email enquiry@sraccountax.ca. Do not include passwords, tax credentials, identity documents or sensitive financial records in ordinary email or the public form.
