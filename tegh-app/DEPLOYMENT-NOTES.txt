Verify after upload
- Sign in as the Platform Owner; the sidebar shows the new TEGH brand block and
  one-line menu labels (Banking: Match and Post, Transactions Report, Bank
  General Ledger, Bank Reconciliation).
- Receivables > Invoice Register: tick one invoice; View / Record Payment /
  Credit Note actions appear above the table.
- Banking > Reconcile Account: the bank balance is calculated (opening balance +
  imported transactions); the reconciliation statement lists reconciling items;
  Complete is enabled only when the unexplained difference is $0.00.
- Credit & Debit Notes: the PDF button downloads a note PDF.
- Payroll > CRA Remittance: choosing a period end prefills tax, CPP and EI.
- Settings > Backup: a new backup downloads as .tegh; an older backup made
  before the product rename still restores.
- Dashboard: a one-sentence summary and an Ask box appear above the cards
  ("Money in the bank", "Customers owe you", "You owe suppliers", "Profit this
  year"), and To Do lists only what needs doing.
- Settings > Appearance: switch to Dark and to Top navigation; menus, reports
  and forms stay readable.
- Banking > Match and Post: the Bank Transactions header shows one bank
  balance for the To date (red when negative).
- Open an existing customer: the screen says Edit Customer and, if no opening
  balance exists, offers Post opening balance.
- Banking > Transactions Report: select one pending line; Edit Transaction
  opens a form, and saving updates the row. Posted lines have no Edit button.
- Payroll Support (sidebar): the Payroll Support Tool notice shows at the top;
  the employee form has no SIN field and suggests an Employee ID (EMP-0001);
  finalizing a pay run needs the "I have reviewed and verified…" checkbox.
- On a phone: open ☰ and tap Banking. The Banking page (Activity and Reports)
  opens and the menu closes; no submenu expands in the menu.
- Dashboard: a fifth card shows "GST/HST you owe" (or "refund due"); its ↗ opens
  the GST/HST Summary.
- Settings > Team and access > Client Viewing Links: create a link for a test
  email you can read, open it in a private window, click "Email me a code",
  enter the code and check the dashboard and a report download. Email
  delivery must be configured (mail.from_email and SMTP) for codes to arrive.
- R133 creates client_view_links, client_view_codes and client_view_sessions
  automatically on first use (CREATE privilege needed).
- R134: upload client-view.html, api/client_view_r133.php and the three
  assets/tegh-client-view-* files together. The client dashboard shows a chart
  for each shared report that has one.
- R130 clears SINs saved by earlier releases the first time each company opens
  Payroll Support (recorded in its audit trail as payroll.sin_data_removed).
  To clear every company at once instead, run:
  UPDATE payroll_employees SET sin_ciphertext=NULL, sin_last_four=NULL;
  Older backups and database dumps taken before R130 may still contain SINs;
  store or delete them according to your retention policy.
- R135 (BC, MB, QC or SK company registered for PST): a taxable invoice line to
  a customer in the same province shows "Charge PST"; totals show GST/HST and
  PST separately; after issuing, the journal credits 2100 and 2110 separately.
  Settings > Company Details shows "How sales tax works for this company".
  Reports > Product Activity replaces "Inventory Report".
- R136 (Quebec company): Company Details PST rate shows 9.975 (re-enter 9.975
  and save if it shows 9.98). A $1,000 taxable invoice to a Quebec customer
  shows QST $99.75 and posts $99.75 to 2110.
- R137: Settings > Company Setup > Tax Codes lists codes. Add a code for your
  province (Start from a Canadian example fills it in), save, then create an
  invoice for a customer in that province: the line's Tax code defaults to it
  and the totals list each tax. Check the journal posts each tax to its GL.
  Saving the first code switches an existing company to tax codes.
- In the browser console: window.TeghPortal exists.
- Tegh Assist (top bar, also on phones): ask "who owes me money?" and the
  answer starts with a plain sentence ("Customers owe you $…") in about a second; "pay my supplier" opens Vendor
  Payments; "can I deduct my car" gives a plain answer.
