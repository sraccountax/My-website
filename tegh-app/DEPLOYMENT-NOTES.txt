This package contains every release through R161 (R118–R161 change notes are
included). R161 is the private beta package (it replaces R160): fixes from the final functional test with large companies in every mode (simultaneous saves, large registers, a background check cancelling loading screens, amounts under the scrollbar, badge and count formatting; see R161-CHANGES.md); no database change. R160: the platform owner's Invitations & Access page is reorganised into Invite someone / Sent invitations / Sign-up & email; an invitation fits the person (a new address can get its own workspace, the inviter's companies or both; an existing login only gets access to more companies; a deactivated or deleted login is restored instead, and the person gets a password email), and api/.htaccess sets RewriteBase so /api/ routes work on IONOS (see R160-CHANGES.md); no database change. R159: every new company has its own onboarding with an empty chart; owners can archive a company; permanent deletion is a request that Tegh support (the platform owner) approves (see R159-CHANGES.md); the columns companies.archived_at and archived_by and the table company_deletion_requests are added on first use. R158: deleting a company works again, and the host backup scripts in the Beta Kit wait for running requests, detect any edited record and stop on any database error (see R158-CHANGES.md); no database change. R157: new users are taken through company onboarding (six steps; modules stay locked until the owner marks every step complete), followed by a Tegh Assist tour; companies have a time zone; Quick Actions show their keys; the theme switch is in the profile menu; Document Intake and the PDF statement reader are marked Beta. R156: the Terms carry the beta conditions, the Terms and Privacy pages show the operator details from config.php, and invitations are refused until those details are complete. It also fixes DEF-18: Match and Post, report periods and default dates now use the accounting (Toronto) date even when the browser's own date is already the next day. R155: the terms version recorded at invitation acceptance matches the published pages, and an optional beta notice shows in the top bar (config app.beta_notice). R154 lets the GL Account Report, the Bank GL Report and Budgets run for several selected companies. R153 adds the company address and short name, company labels on multi-company report lines, sidebar arrows and the theme icons. R152 reads more statement layouts and multi-statement PDFs, and reads invoice lines with supplier memory, suggestions and duplicate checks. R151 brings one form for invoices, credit notes and debit notes, itemized vendor invoices, Edit on the main screen and the scrolling fixes. R150 keeps the public pages in the light design on dark-mode devices. R149 replaces the invitation link with an invitation code. R148 adds the Sign in / Create account tabs with account creation through an invitation link. R147 brings the owner's public-page update and opens the home page at the site root. R146 fixes the R145 OCR clean-up on clean scans (DEF-16). R145 improves the PDF statement converter and Document Intake. R144 adds Tegh Intelligence (no external AI).
