Availability is not jurisdiction support

Use TEGH worldwide. Check the feature boundary.

Access

The web application is intended to be reachable worldwide, subject to local law, internet access and service restrictions.

Currencies

Books support CAD, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD and INR. Currency availability does not supply local tax or filing rules.

Accounting & tax

General accounting can be used in multiple currencies. Canadian GST/HST/PST workflows are the specifically implemented tax focus.

Payroll

Payroll calculations target Canadian jurisdictions outside Quebec. TEGH does not provide worldwide payroll, filing or remittance support.
