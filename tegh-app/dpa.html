Customer Data Processing Addendum

Draft allocation when a customer instructs TEGH to process data.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-21-beta.1 · Contact: enquiry@sraccountax.ca

1. Roles

The customer determines purposes for customer accounting, payroll, employee, supplier, customer, document and Dashboard data. SR Accountax processes it under service instructions. Each party controls its independent information.

2. Instructions and confidentiality

TEGH processes customer data to deliver, secure, support, back up, export and close the service and as law requires. Authorized personnel are role-limited. Customers must not instruct unlawful or unnecessary processing.

3. Security and assistance

TEGH maintains proportionate safeguards and assists, considering processing nature, with rights requests, incidents and assessments. Customers remain responsible for user access, recipient selection, notices and professional review.

4. Providers/transfers

Providers are listed in Subprocessors. IONOS US hosting is confirmed direction. Material additions need notice and an objection route. Required cross-border assessments/contracts/mechanisms depend on customer jurisdiction.

5. Incidents

TEGH will notify customers without undue delay after confirming a breach affecting their data when legally/contractually required and provide available assessment information. No universal Canadian 72-hour rule is stated.

6. Return, deletion, audit

Closure uses the approved export/retention schedule, legal holds and backup expiry. Reasonable security information can support review without exposing other customers or secrets. On-site audit, liability, transfer annexes and legal contracting name need legal review.
