Payroll Support Tool

Keep payroll records and accounting in the same workspace.

The Payroll Support Tool helps you prepare and review payroll for supported Canadian payroll scenarios, with province selection and payroll registers. It is for Canadian payroll outside Quebec; Quebec payroll (Revenu Québec, QPP, QPIP) is not supported. You review and confirm every result before relying on it or filing. Tegh does not store SINs, make direct deposits, produce ROEs or T4 slips/XML, or send payments or returns to CRA. Remittance records document payments made outside Tegh.

Tegh Intelligence

Your books, with the next question made clearer.

Built into Tegh with no external AI calls. Rules and statistics explain what they find; a person decides what to do.

Tegh brief

A dashboard summary of cash and its 30-day change, overdue customers, vendor invoices due, GST/HST, bank review and year-to-date profit.

Worth a look

Review possible duplicate invoices, unusually large vendor invoices, regional tax-code mismatches, parked balances, stale bank lines, overdrawn accounts and unposted verified pay runs. Open a source or dismiss a finding.

Who to chase first

Rank overdue customers by amount owed, days late and recent payment habits. Open Collection Drafts to prepare and review a reminder.

Cash runway

See cash against historical monthly bank movement, a six-month chart with a table view and a what-if monthly cost. This is a historical estimate; Cash Forecast provides a forward view.

Bank suggestions

Similar earlier postings and learned rules suggest an account and tax code with a reason and confidence. Use suggestion fills the fields; you review the preview and post.

Ask your books

Ask Tegh Assist about spending by account or vendor, sales versus last year, biggest expenses or top customers for a named period. Answers include the figures and period used.

Documents & sales tax

From source document to reviewed accounting.

Read, check and prepare the record before saving or posting it.

Bank Statement Converter

Read text PDF statements by column, review dates, descriptions and amounts, and check opening, running and closing balances and page totals when available. Unfamiliar layouts may need correction; scanned statements need the bank’s CSV or OFX.

Document Intake

Extract candidate fields locally in your browser from PDF invoices, scanned PDFs and receipt photos. Review dates, vendor, line items and subtotal + tax = total. French invoice labels are recognised; OCR uses an English model. Nothing is posted automatically.

Credit & debit notes

Return eligible items at their original price or add adjustment lines, save a draft or post against the original invoice, and download a note PDF. Excess returns are blocked and credits retain their remaining balance.

Configurable tax codes

Set named tax components, exact rates and separate sales and purchase GL accounts by country or province/state. Canadian starter codes cover provinces and territories, including GST/HST and PST/RST/QST. Review registration, regional applicability and exemptions; foreign rates are entered and verified by you.

Sales tax reporting & settlements

Use the Tax Code Report and GST/HST reports to inspect activity. Record supported remittances or refunds through protected tax workflows, including recoverable tax clearing.

Product Activity

Review quantities and amounts on customer and vendor invoices. Tegh does not track stock on hand, cost layers, cost of goods sold or inventory valuation; inventory adjustments belong in the general ledger.

Client viewing links

Let clients see the result of your work.

Company owners and admins choose what each client can view. Clients do not need a Tegh account.

Dashboard & selected reports

Share selected financial statements, AR/AP ageing, invoice registers, expenses, General Ledger Detail, GST/HST and Bank Reconciliation. Dashboard charts follow the reports shared and offer table views.

An expiring link and emailed code

Choose a lifetime from 7 days to 1 year. A code sent to the client’s mailbox opens a view-only session. Clients can change report dates and download PDF or Excel; they cannot change the books.

Control and audit history

Change shared reports, issue a replacement link or turn access off. Sign-ins, report views and downloads are audited. Payroll, audit history and working papers cannot be shared.

Accounting & management

Keep the detail close to the daily work.

Guided Accounting and Full Accounting use the same books, posting services and company permissions.

Advanced accounting

Manage fixed assets and depreciation, budgets and actuals, recurring transactions, expense vouchers, period locks and month-end review checks.

Forecasts & management reports

Review Cash Forecast, upcoming payments, expected collections, financial analysis and GIFI mappings and reports. Dashboard Figures lets you choose the GL accounts behind summary cards.

Native review workflows

Use the Tegh Agent Centre and month-end checklists to review findings and open source workflows. Collection Drafts require review, approval and an explicit send action.

Foreign currency records

Work with supported foreign-currency bank and document workflows and exchange differences. The base and reporting currency is CAD for every company, including companies outside Canada.

Exports & saved downloads

Export supported reports and registers to PDF, Excel or CSV, choose readable columns and periods, and retrieve saved output under Requested Downloads.

Separate company files

Switch company files with role-based access, audit history and company-scoped imports. Download backups and grant time-limited support access when needed.
