Security Statement

Evidence-based safeguards and beta limitations.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-21-beta.1 · Contact: enquiry@sraccountax.ca

Application safeguards

Server-side company/role authorization, password hashing, MFA-capable login, invalidatable sessions, CSRF/same-origin checks, prepared queries, output escaping, upload validation, private paths, rate limits, recipient links/codes/sessions and audit events are implemented.

Accounting integrity

Balanced posting, protected controls, period locks, idempotency, reversal/audit history, tax mapping and reconciliation remain intact. Client acknowledgements cannot post entries or authorize payments.

Hosting and recovery

Production hosting is planned on IONOS in the United States. TLS, private storage, database privileges, scheduled/off-site backups and isolated restoration require deployment verification. Local synthetic recovery is not production evidence.

Administration

The owner authorizes only Ravinder Singh for production administration. Providers, automated systems and lawful authorities may still have required access. Admin MFA/account inventory/provider access must be checked after deployment.

Report concerns

Email enquiry@sraccountax.ca with “Security report.” Do not send secrets, identity documents, customer data or exploit traffic. Do not test without written authorization.

Limitations

No SOC 2/ISO certification, end-to-end encryption, external penetration-test result, uptime guarantee or production-readiness claim is made.
