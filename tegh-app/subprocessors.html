Purpose: web, database and private files. Country: United States, owner-confirmed direction. Open: exact contracting entity/region, backups and support access must be verified in the production account.

Transactional account, MFA, Dashboard and support messages require SMTP. No production provider/country is established in this repository. Do not activate one until identity, location, retention, security and contract are recorded.
