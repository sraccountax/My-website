Ask your books

Plain questions. Figures from your ledger.

Supported book questions cover spending by expense account or vendor, sales, biggest expenses and top customers for a named period.

Everyday language

Try “am I making money this year?”, “what bills are due this week?” or “upload my bank statement”. Reports without dates use a labelled, editable default period.

Guidance with a next step

Built-in guidance explains common Canadian bookkeeping and tax questions and links to the relevant screen. Confirm general guidance with CRA or your accountant.

Know what to review

“Anything unusual?” opens Tegh Intelligence. Its findings use rules and statistics, can miss issues or raise false alarms, and do not change accounting records.

Built for control

Tegh can help. You stay in control.

The assistant is intentionally not a second accounting engine.

Permissions first

Server-side company roles determine what a user can read, prepare or execute.

Bound confirmations

Financial and destructive confirmations stay tied to the current action, company, arguments and records.

Result-set identity

“These” refers to the visible/selected current result rather than an undefined historical set.

Audit trail

Supported actions can record that they were initiated through Ask Tegh while using the same underlying service.

Company scope

Switching company invalidates or revalidates state that should not cross company boundaries.

Safer uncertainty

If a financial command is ambiguous, Tegh should clarify rather than commit a materially different action.

A workspace, not a chatbot

Conversation on one side. Real Tegh results on the other.

The companion workspace lets you inspect transactions or reports while continuing to give instructions.
