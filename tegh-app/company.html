Company

Accounting software should feel calm, clear and accountable.

Tegh is being built around the idea that financial software can become easier to use without hiding the accounting controls professionals rely on.

Why Tegh

Control. Clarity. Intelligence.

Tegh brings day-to-day finance work into one connected accounting workspace. The product is designed for business owners who want understandable workflows and for accounting professionals who still need ledgers, journals, Trial Balance, source traceability and role controls.

The visual and interaction philosophy is called Quiet Financial Intelligence: the interface should stay out of the way until the user needs detail, then make the next action and the accounting consequence clear.

Tegh Intelligence adds a brief from your books, findings with reasons, collection priorities and cash-runway estimates using built-in rules and statistics without external AI calls. Tegh Assist answers supported questions from the posted ledger and opens the next workflow.

The recurring design motif—the Living Ledger—represents the connection between source activity, accounting entries and financial statements.

Principles

How Tegh should behave.

These principles guide both product design and accounting-system decisions.

Make the task clearer.

If a design element does not make the financial task clearer, calmer or faster, it should not compete for attention.

Do not bypass accounting controls.

Imports, Tegh Assist and bulk workflows should still use the same validated services as manual work.

Keep actions traceable.

Important changes should remain tied to the company, user, source and accounting result.
